Reflation, not Goldilocks — growth re-accelerating into sticky inflation

The US is in Reflation — growth accelerating, inflation re-accelerating. GDP printed +3.4% in Q3, manufacturing hit a 26-month high (ISM 55.5), and payrolls added 165k. But Core PCE re-accelerated to 3.1% (up from 3.0%), the Oct 28 FOMC was a 10-2 vote to hold with two dissenters wanting a hike, and real wages are now negative (+3.1% AHE vs 3.7% CPI). The market is positioned for Stagflation (defensive rotation into healthcare and utilities) but the data says Reflation. That dislocation is the opportunity — cyclicals and energy are under-owned relative to what the data supports. The tail risk: if growth rolls while inflation stays sticky, the regime tips into Stagflation. The 10Y at 5.15% is the clearest signal that markets are not pricing a soft landing.

European Sovereign Spreads vs Bund

🚨 France sovereign crisis — OAT-Bund at 156bp
What is happening: France's 10Y OAT spread to German Bunds has widened to 156bp — a level not seen since the 2012 eurozone debt crisis peak. France is the second-largest eurozone economy (€2.8T GDP) and its spread blowout matters structurally. The proximate causes: Prime Minister Barnier's minority government is unable to pass the 2025 budget; a no-confidence vote is imminent; fiscal projections show a 6.1% deficit-to-GDP vs a 3% EU Stability Pact ceiling; and France's public debt is on course to hit 112% of GDP by 2026. The ECB's TPI (Transmission Protection Instrument) would theoretically cap spreads, but France's fiscal slippage makes TPI eligibility politically fraught — Lagarde cannot be seen rescuing a country that refuses to consolidate. This is not a 2010-style crisis (no bank run, no sudden stop) but a slow-motion credibility loss that is repricing French risk structurally higher.

Hard rule
IPS pending
Investment policy statement required first
WhatNo screen output includes a position size recommendation. All sizing decisions are deferred until an Investment Policy Statement (IPS) is agreed and in place.
WhyWithout a risk budget, concentration limits, and return objectives defined, any sizing is arbitrary. The screens identify what; the IPS defines how much.
DoOutputs are call type (Buy / Watchlist / Pass / No action), reference price, entry levels, key assumption, falsifier, next catalyst, review date — not dollar amounts or percentages.

All names
15% p.a.

WhatNo name is advanced from watchlist to buy without a credible path to 15% per year over five years. Applied to all eight screens equally.
WhyThe hurdle filters out low-conviction ideas that may screen well on a technical signal but do not offer adequate return for the risk of active management vs index ownership.
